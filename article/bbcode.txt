[b][size=120]BPOS — Point of Sales for OpenCart[/size][/b]

[b]Selling online and in-store? You shouldn't have to manage two separate systems.[/b]

Imagine this:

A customer buys the last item from your website. A few minutes later, someone walks into your physical store and asks for the same product.

But your cashier doesn't know it's already sold.

Now you have an oversold product, an unhappy customer, and another problem to fix.

This happens when your online store and physical shop operate separately.

[b]BPOS solves this by turning your existing OpenCart store into a complete Point of Sale system.[/b]

Your website and physical counter use the same products, stock, customers, orders, taxes, coupons, and payment methods. When your cashier completes a sale, it's recorded directly in OpenCart as a normal order.

No second product catalog. No separate inventory to update. No extra system to reconcile.

Simply open your POS address (for example, yourstore.com/pos), sign in, and start selling.

[b][size=120]Is BPOS Right for Your Business?[/size][/b]

BPOS is designed for businesses that sell both online and in person.

[list]
[*] Retail stores that want website and counter sales to share the same inventory.
[*] Boutiques and shops where cashiers need a simple, fast checkout.
[*] Business owners tired of maintaining products and stock in two different systems.
[*] Growing businesses with multiple branches, cashiers, and checkout terminals.
[/list]

[b][size=120]1. Get Customers Through Checkout Faster[/size][/b]

Your cashier shouldn't have to navigate several pages just to complete a sale. Find products, manage the cart, and finish checkout from one screen.

[list]
[*] [b]Find Products Quickly:[/b] Search by product name, model, or SKU, or scan a barcode using a USB or Bluetooth scanner. Exact barcode matches can be added automatically.
[*] [b]Choose Your Product View:[/b] Use Compact layout to display more products or Gallery layout for larger thumbnails. Configure each terminal separately.
[*] [b]Apply Discounts and Coupons:[/b] Use OpenCart coupons, add extra charges, and set maximum discount limits for cashiers.
[*] [b]Handle Customers Easily:[/b] Search, select, or create customers without leaving the sales screen. Guest checkout is also supported.
[*] [b]Handle Delivery Orders:[/b] Select any enabled OpenCart shipping method when a customer needs an order delivered.
[*] [b]Know What's Left:[/b] Display low-stock warnings at your chosen threshold and follow your OpenCart stock settings.
[*] [b]Switch Currencies:[/b] Allow cashiers to change between enabled store currencies (optional, per device).
[/list]

[b][size=120]2. Accept Payments Using Your Existing Methods[/size][/b]

You don't need to configure another payment system just for your physical store.

[list]
[*] [b]Cash:[/b] Enter the amount received and let BPOS calculate the change.
[*] [b]OpenCart Payment Extensions:[/b] Use your enabled OpenCart payment methods. Offline methods such as bank transfer and Cash on Delivery simply record the order. Online gateways depend on the extension, so test yours before going live.
[/list]

[b][size=120]3. Print Receipts That Match Your Store[/size][/b]

Give customers a proper receipt after every sale, using the printer setup that works for your business.

[list]
[*] Print through the browser dialog, or send ESC/POS directly to 58 mm and 80 mm thermal printers over USB, Bluetooth, or the RawBT app on Android. USB and Bluetooth printing needs Chrome or Edge (Chrome on Android for Bluetooth).
[*] Support for standard 58 mm and 80 mm receipt paper.
[*] Automatically print after checkout, with optional paper cutting and a test-print function.
[*] Choose what appears on your receipts: logo, store address, phone, cashier, terminal, customer details, tax breakdown, and order barcode.
[*] Translate receipt labels for each language and preview the result directly in settings.
[/list]

[b][size=120]4. Keep Every Sale Accounted For[/size][/b]

Need to check an order, process a refund, or cancel an incorrect transaction? Manage it directly from your POS.

[list]
[*] Browse orders from the last 7 days or choose a custom date range.
[*] View order details and sales KPIs.
[*] Process refunds and void orders according to user permissions.
[*] Every refund and void action leaves a note on the corresponding OpenCart order.
[*] Export order records to CSV or Excel.
[/list]

[b][size=120]5. Manage Products and Print Barcode Labels[/size][/b]

Prepare products for checkout without switching to another management tool.

[list]
[*] Add and edit products directly from POS.
[*] Control which products appear in the POS and filter by category or stock.
[*] Automatically generate missing barcodes using product SKU or Model.
[*] Print product labels on 58 mm / 80 mm rolls or A4 sheets.
[*] Adjust label columns, margins, height, and barcode width, with quick presets and print preview.
[*] Select multiple products and print labels together.
[/list]

[b][size=120]6. Know Your Customers Better[/size][/b]

Your customer information and purchase history stay connected to OpenCart.

[list]
[*] See total customers, new customers in the last 30 days, repeat customers, and inactive customers.
[*] Search by name, phone, or email.
[*] Filter customers by customer group.
[*] View purchase history, total spending, and internal notes.
[*] Optionally create an OpenCart account for new customers.
[/list]

[b][size=120]7. See How Your Business Is Performing[/size][/b]

Don't just count transactions. Understand where your sales come from and what needs attention.

[list]
[*] Track net sales, transactions, average order value, items sold, discounts, refunds, and voids.
[*] Compare performance with the previous period.
[*] View daily sales charts and breakdowns by payment method and product category.
[*] See how much each cashier sells.
[*] Compare sales between stores and identify products running low on stock.
[*] Track coupon usage, shipping, and extra-charge figures.
[*] Export reports for further analysis.
[/list]

[b][size=120]8. Give Your Team the Right Access[/size][/b]

Not every cashier needs access to every operation. Decide what each person can see and do.

[list]
[*] [b]Four User Roles:[/b] Cashier, Supervisor, Manager, and Owner, each with designated pages.
[*] [b]Adjustable Permissions:[/b] Control who can apply discounts, add charges, use coupons, process refunds, void orders, and export data.
[*] [b]Quick PIN Sign-In:[/b] Allow cashiers to switch users using a 4–6 digit PIN.
[*] [b]Multiple Sign-In Options:[/b] Username and password, PIN, or an existing OpenCart admin session.
[*] [b]Login Protection:[/b] Account lockouts after repeated failed attempts and IP-based rate limiting.
[*] [b]Activity Records:[/b] Audit log and sliding 12-hour sessions.
[/list]

[b][size=120]9. Manage Multiple Branches and Checkout Counters[/size][/b]

Expanding to another location shouldn't mean managing another POS system.

[list]
[*] Use your existing OpenCart stores as branches.
[*] Assign cashiers to individual stores.
[*] Register each counter or tablet as a separate terminal.
[*] Check whether terminals are online or offline.
[*] Configure printers, product layouts, and barcode scanner options for each terminal.
[/list]
